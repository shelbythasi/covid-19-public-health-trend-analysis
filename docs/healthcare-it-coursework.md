# Healthcare IT Coursework Exposure

## Context

This document summarizes healthcare information technology topics studied through **ISM 4041: Ethics in Information Technology** at the University of South Florida.

These concepts are included as **coursework-based domain exposure** and are not presented as professional experience administering clinical systems or managing healthcare compliance programs.

## Electronic Health Records (EHR)

Coursework examined how electronic health records support the storage and sharing of patient information across healthcare organizations. Topics included the relationship between EHR systems, clinical workflows, computerized provider order entry, scheduling, billing, and decision support.

A healthcare case study on Kaiser Permanente's HealthConnect system was used to examine how an integrated EHR can support care coordination, access to patient information, medication alerts, and organizational performance measurement.

## HIPAA & Health Data Privacy

Coursework covered the role of the **Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)** in healthcare information privacy and security.

Key concepts included:

- Protection of patient health information
- Privacy and security responsibilities of healthcare organizations
- Risks associated with unauthorized access or misuse of health data
- Responsibilities involving healthcare partners and third parties
- Ethical considerations surrounding access to electronic patient records

## Clinical Decision Support

Coursework examined **clinical decision support (CDS)** as the use of clinical knowledge and patient-specific information to support healthcare decision-making.

Healthcare diagnosis case studies explored how decision-support systems can combine patient history, physical findings, laboratory information, and medical reference sources to assist clinicians in evaluating possible diagnoses.

## Telehealth

Coursework covered **telehealth and telemedicine** as the use of telecommunications and information technology to deliver healthcare services, education, and administrative support across distance.

Topics included:

- Live telemedicine
- Remote monitoring
- Store-and-forward telemedicine
- Use of digital information and communication technologies to expand access to care
